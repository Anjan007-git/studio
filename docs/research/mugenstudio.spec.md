# Mugen Studio Reverse-Engineering Specification

## Site Overview
- **Target URL:** `https://mugenstudio.framer.website/`
- **Site Key:** `mugenstudio`
- **Page Key:** `root`
- **Framer Template:** Mugen Design Studio Framer Template
- **Color Scheme:** Dark mode (#050505 background, #0e0e0e card surface, #ffffff primary text, #828282 muted text, #262626 borders)
- **Typography:** Geist / Inter / Manrope grotesque sans-serif with tight letter-spacing
- **Framer Features:** Lenis smooth scroll, live time display, interactive pricing monthly/quarterly toggle, accordion FAQ, copy-to-clipboard CTA

6. **Services [03]:** 5 detailed service cards (Brand Identity, Digital Design, Product Design, Marketing & Growth, Development).
7. **Process [04]:** 5 sprint steps (01 Listen & Learn, 02 Strategy First, 03 Design in Sprints, 04 Refine Until Right, 05 Launch & Learn) + turnaround badges.
8. **Pricing [05]:** Built to scale, monthly / quarterly switcher (15% off), 3 tiers (Growth, Scale, Custom Project), plus PM advisory note.
9. **Testimonials:** Verified reviews from VP of Product, Founder, and Design Leads.
10. **FAQ:** Accordions for retainer questions, roll-over hours, start times, and team structure.
11. **Articles:** 3 insights with tags and publication dates.
12. **CTA & Footer:** Copy email action, newsletter subscription form, copyright Toronto (CA), and site navigation.
